How the knobs work

  • Temperature. Logits are divided by T before softmax — T < 1 sharpens the leader, T > 1 lifts the whole field.
  • Top-k. Everything below rank k is deleted before the wheel is built.
  • Top-p. The wheel keeps only the shortest head of the ranking that covers p of the mass.

Data & sources

Real next-token logits from GPT-2 117M, computed by a verified numpy forward pass on four handcrafted prompts: up to 16,384 top candidates per prompt plus the remaining tail as a 64-bin logit histogram. The three continuations are precomputed greedy and seeded samples. Runs fully offline.
